## ✨ What's Inside?

A fully dynamic, two-page portal designed for both our community members and club executives. 

### 🟢 For the Community (User View)
- **Club Overview**: Discover who we are and what we stand for.
- **Event Gallery**: Relive our best moments from campus hackathons like **GAMEVERSE** and **APPATHETICS 3.0**.
- **Meet the Team**: Get to know the current executives, leads, and the entire core team driving the club forward.

### 🔴 For the Executives (Admin Dashboard)
- **No-Code Updates**: Edit website content directly from the admin dashboard—no coding required!
- **Dynamic Team Management**: Seamlessly add new post holders, update roles, or remove past members when the tenure changes.
- **Live Event Announcements**: Push new event details and pictures instantly.
- **Instant Sync**: Everything updates in real-time.
